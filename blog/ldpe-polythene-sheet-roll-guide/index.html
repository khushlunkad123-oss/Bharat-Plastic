Section 01

What Is a Polythene Sheet Roll?

A polythene sheet roll — also called a plastic sheet roll, polythene covering sheet, or simply a roll of plastic — is a wide, flat film of low density polyethylene wound tightly around a core. Unlike polythene tubing (which forms a tube), a sheet roll is a true flat film: open on both edges, ready to unroll and lay flat across any surface. It is supplied in rolls of varying width, length, and thickness and can be cut to any size on site.

The word polythene is the common name for polyethylene in India and the UK. Specifically, LDPE (Low Density Polyethylene) is the grade used for most flexible sheet rolls — valued for its softness, clarity, high elongation before tearing, and outstanding moisture barrier properties.

Section 02

Why LDPE? The Material Behind the Roll

Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE) — also written as polyethylene polythene in common industrial use — is a thermoplastic polymer produced by radical polymerisation of ethylene gas. Its branched molecular structure gives it a lower crystallinity than HDPE, which translates directly into the properties that make it ideal for sheet rolls: exceptional softness, high elongation (it stretches before it tears), and the ability to be sealed with heat.

Crucially, LDPE plastic is transparent to milky-clear, making it easy to see what is underneath it. It does not absorb moisture and provides an excellent barrier against water vapour — exactly what you need for damp-proofing, concrete curing, or protecting goods from humidity.

Section 03

Where Is a Polythene Sheet Roll Used?

The polythene sheet roll has a staggering range of applications — from protecting freshly poured concrete on a construction site to covering a machine in a factory floor. Below are the most common industrial and commercial uses across India.

🏗️

Construction — Floor & Surface Protection

Laid under concrete during curing to retain moisture, used as plastic sheet for floor protection during building work, and as a dust/debris barrier between work zones. 150–250 µm recommended for site use.

💧

Damp-Proof Membranes (DPM)

Placed under floor screeds and concrete slabs to prevent rising damp. A key application of construction plastic sheeting in civil and residential projects. Typically 250–300 µm heavy gauge.

🌾

Agriculture — Mulch & Greenhouse Cover

Black polythene sheet is used for mulching to suppress weeds, retain soil moisture, and warm the root zone. Clear sheets are used as greenhouse roof films and crop protection covers.

🏭

Industrial — Machine & Equipment Covers

Rolls of plastic covering sheet are used to drape over machinery, pallets, and equipment during storage, transport, or maintenance — protecting against dust, humidity, and contamination.

📦

Packaging Liners & Pallet Covers

Wide polythene roll film lines shipping containers, pallet bases, and packaging boxes to provide a moisture barrier. Widely used in FMCG, pharma, and export packaging operations.

🎨

Painters & Decorators — Masking

A key use of plastic on roll in finishing trades: masking floors, furniture, fixtures, and surfaces before painting or polishing. Lightweight, cuts easily with a knife, does not leave residue.

❄️

Cold Storage & Refrigeration Lining

LDPE remains flexible at low temperatures (unlike many other plastics), making polythene sheet rolls ideal as vapour barriers and liners in cold rooms, freezer units, and chilled warehouses.

🌊

Pond & Canal Lining

Heavy-gauge polythene sheets (200–300 µm) are used to line small ponds, water reservoirs, and irrigation canals — providing a cost-effective, flexible waterproof lining that conforms to irregular ground.

Thickness Guide — Which Micron Should You Choose?

Thickness is the single most important specification when buying a polythene sheet roll. In India, thickness is measured in microns (µm), where 1 micron = 0.001 mm. The minimum legal thickness for plastic sheeting in Maharashtra is 50 microns under the Plastic Waste Management Rules — thinner products are non-compliant.

Sheet Roll vs. Polythene Tubing — Which Do You Need?

Buyers often confuse polythene sheet rolls with polythene tubing rolls. The difference is simple:

A polythene sheet roll is flat film, open on both sides — best for laying flat as a covering sheet, floor protection, or lining. A polythene tubing roll is a continuous tube (seamless, no side seams) that you cut and heat-seal to make custom-length bags. If you're covering a floor, a machine, or using it as a damp-proof membrane — choose the sheet roll. If you're making bags for components or storage — choose the tubing roll.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a polythene sheet roll used for?

+
LDPE polythene sheet rolls are used as protective floor covering in construction, damp-proof membranes (DPM), greenhouse film, mulching sheets in agriculture, machine covers, packaging liners, dust-proof covers, and concrete curing sheets. Their flexibility and moisture resistance make them ideal for both industrial and agricultural applications.

What thickness of polythene sheet roll should I buy?

+
For light-duty indoor coverage, 50–75 microns is sufficient. Construction floor protection requires 100–150 microns. Heavy-duty applications like damp-proof membranes, pond liners, and long-term outdoor covers need 200–300 microns. In Maharashtra, the minimum legal thickness for plastic sheeting is 50 microns under the Plastic Waste Management Rules 2016.

What is the difference between a polythene sheet roll and polythene tubing?

+
A polythene sheet roll is a flat, single-layer film — open on both sides — supplied as a wide roll to lay flat as a covering or protective sheet. A polythene tubing roll is a seamless tube (lay-flat) that you cut to length and heat-seal to make bags. If you need a floor or machinery covering, choose a sheet roll. If you need to make bags for components or storage, choose the tubing roll.

Can I get polythene sheet rolls in black colour?

+
Yes. Bharat Plastic Manufacturing Co. supplies polythene sheet rolls in natural (clear/milky), black, and other custom colours. Black polythene is widely used for agriculture mulching, root zone temperature management, and light-blocking applications. Black polythene sheet rolls are available in all standard thicknesses and widths.

Is LDPE plastic safe for food contact use?

+
Virgin LDPE is generally recognised as food-safe (FDA, EU food contact regulations). However, any food-contact application should be specified at the time of ordering so that appropriate food-grade additives and virgin-only raw material is used. Always confirm food-contact suitability with the manufacturer when ordering for this purpose.
