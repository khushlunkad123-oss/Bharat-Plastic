Section 01

What Exactly Is a Polythene Tubing Roll?

A polythene tubing roll — interchangeably called lay-flat polythene tubing, poly tubing roll, or a plastic tube roll — is a continuous tube of LDPE film, wound onto a core like a bobbin. When flattened (laid flat), it looks like a flat strip of plastic. When opened, it forms a seamless tube — with no side seams. Buyers cut it to whatever length they need and heat-seal one or both ends, instantly creating custom-sized bags.

This is the key reason buyers choose polyethylene tubing over pre-made bags: total freedom over bag length. Whether you need a 6-inch sleeve for a small component or a 3-foot cover for a steel pipe, the same roll serves you. That flexibility is what makes it indispensable across industries from industrial plastic packaging to agricultural film.

Section 02

Why Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE)?

The choice of material matters enormously in plastic production. Bharat Plastic manufactures its polythene tubing rolls from low density polyethylene — commonly written as LDPE or LDPE plastic. Understanding this material explains why the product performs so well.

Low density polyethylene (LDPE) is a thermoplastic polymer made from ethylene monomer. Its "low density" designation (typically 0.910–0.940 g/cm³) reflects its highly branched molecular structure, which prevents polymer chains from packing tightly. This gives LDPE film its characteristic flexibility, softness, and toughness — even at low temperatures. Unlike rigid plastics, LDPE tubing can be stretched, twisted, and squeezed without cracking, making it ideal for wrapping irregular shapes.

"Low density polyethylene is one of the most widely used polyethylene grades in the world — and for good reason. Its flexibility, clarity, moisture resistance, and low cost make it the workhorse of the plastic packaging industry."

Section 04

Custom Sizes, Specifications & What to Order

When ordering polythene tubing rolls from Bharat Plastic, buyers typically specify four parameters. Understanding each one ensures you get exactly the poly bag or tube sleeve your operation needs.

1. Lay-Flat Width

The lay-flat width is the width of the flattened tube as it comes off the roll. When you open the tube, the resulting bag or sleeve will be approximately twice the lay-flat width in circumference. For example, a 6-inch lay-flat tube opens to roughly a 12-inch circumference — useful for enclosing a cylindrical object of about 3.8 inches in diameter. Standard poly tubing sizes range from 2 inches to 200+ inches lay-flat, with custom widths available. Understanding lay-flat width is the single most important specification when purchasing.

2. Plastic Thickness (Microns)

Plastic microns (µm) determine the strength and barrier performance of the film. In Maharashtra, the minimum permissible plastic bag thickness is 50 microns as per plastic waste management rules — Bharat Plastic supplies compliant tubing at this baseline and above. Standard industrial applications typically require 75–150 µm, while heavy-duty uses — like protecting steel or machinery — may need 200–250+ µm. Polythene thickness also affects how the material behaves during heat sealing: thicker film requires longer seal dwell times. Bharat Plastic supplies custom size plastic tubing across this full range.

3. Roll Length & Core Size

Standard tubing roll lengths range from 100 metres to 1,000+ metres depending on thickness and lay-flat width. Longer rolls mean fewer changeovers on a production line. Plastic roll size (core diameter and outer roll diameter) can also be specified for compatibility with your dispensing equipment. For high-volume plastic packaging operations, roll bags plastic (pre-perforated or unperforated) can be wound to meet your exact workflow.

4. Colour & Print

While the default is clear LDPE (fully transparent), coloured polythene or black-opaque versions are also available. For branding, printed poly tubing can carry logos, product names, handling instructions, or barcodes. Custom printed poly tubing is ordered with a minimum quantity and a supplied artwork file. Even basic printed polythene rolls significantly increase the perceived value of products sent to customers.

Buying Polythene Tubing Roll: A Practical Guide for Indian Buyers

Whether you're a small factory in Pune, an agricultural supplier in rural Maharashtra, or an e-commerce warehouse in Mumbai, here's what to look for when sourcing polythene tubing rolls from a plastic manufacturer in India:

Check the LDPE Grade

Not all LDPE plastic is the same. Virgin-grade LDPE produces cleaner, more consistent clear polythene tubing with better heat-seal integrity. Recycled or blended grades may cost less but can introduce contamination, reduced clarity, and inconsistent LDPE film thickness. For industrial and food-contact applications, always ask your plastic bag manufacturer about the material grade. Bharat Plastic uses quality LDPE to ensure consistent ldpe plastic roll output.

Verify Your Specifications in Writing

Before placing a bulk order, confirm all specifications clearly in your purchase order: lay-flat width, thickness in microns, roll length, colour, and any print requirements. A reputable polythene tube manufacturer like Bharat Plastic will confirm these details back to you and flag if any specification is outside standard production range. This simple step avoids mismatches and ensures you get the right plastic tube roll for your operation.

Understand Your Width Requirement

Many buyers confuse "bag width" with "lay-flat width." The lay-flat width of a tube is half the bag's opened circumference. Measure the largest cross-section of your product, add clearance, and divide by π to get the tube diameter you need — then divide that by 2 for the lay-flat width you should order. Poly tubing sizes at Bharat Plastic range from 2 inches lay-flat up to 200+ inches for wide industrial applications.

Consider Minimum Order Quantities

As a micro-scale plastic manufacturer, Bharat Plastic is well-placed to supply smaller quantities than large industrial mills. Whether you need a few rolls for trial or bulk wholesale plastic quantities for ongoing production, reaching out directly ensures you get pricing appropriate to your volume. Ask about polythene roll price, LDPE roll price, and poly bag roll price for your specific width and gauge.

Pro Tip for Small Manufacturers: If you run a packaging operation and regularly use multiple bag sizes, consider switching to a polythene tubing roll system. Frequently Asked Questions

Polythene Tubing Rolls — FAQ

What is the difference between lay-flat tubing and a regular polythene bag?
A lay-flat polythene tubing roll is a continuous tube wound on a core — it has no bottom seal. You cut it to any length and heat-seal the bottom (and optionally the top) to make a bag. A regular polythene bag comes pre-made with a fixed height and is already sealed at the bottom. The tubing roll gives you unlimited flexibility in bag length; the pre-made bag is more convenient but constrained to its fixed size.
What does "lay-flat width" mean and how do I calculate the bag size?
The lay-flat width is the width of the flattened tube as supplied. When opened, the opened bag width equals the lay-flat width on each face — so a 300mm lay-flat tube gives a bag that is 300mm wide. The tube diameter ≈ lay-flat width × 2 ÷ π. Contact Bharat Plastic with your product dimensions and we'll recommend the right poly tubing sizes.
What thickness (microns) should I order for industrial use?
In Maharashtra, plastic waste management rules mandate a minimum thickness of 50 microns — all Bharat Plastic tubing meets this requirement. For standard industrial use (hardware, engineering components, auto parts), 75–150 µm is the typical range. Heavy-duty use (steel profiles, heavy machinery parts, construction) often requires 200–250+ µm LDPE plastic. Bharat Plastic can advise on the right plastic microns for your specific application.
Can polythene tubing rolls be printed with my logo?
Yes. Printed poly tubing — also called custom printed poly tubing or printed polythene rolls — is available with flexographic printing. Supply your artwork and specify the number of colours. Printing requires a minimum order quantity (MOQ) and slightly longer lead times than plain clear polythene tubing. Contact Bharat Plastic for bag printing in Pune quotes and specifications.
What is LDPE and how is it different from HDPE?
Low density polyethylene (LDPE) has a branched molecular structure giving it high flexibility, softness, and ease of heat sealing. High density polyethylene (HDPE) has a more linear structure, making it stiffer, stronger, and less flexible. For lay-flat tubing and flexible packaging, LDPE plastic is preferred because it collapses flat without cracking and seals reliably. HDPE is better for bags requiring higher strength and less flexibility.

Bharat Plastic caters to both small and bulk buyers as a plastic fabrication company in Pune. Minimum order quantities vary by width and thickness. Please contact us directly at bharatplastic.org with your specifications for a custom quote. We work with buyers ranging from small local manufacturers to large industrial packaging operations across Maharashtra.
What is "heat seal tubing" and do I need special equipment?
Heat seal tubing refers to LDPE polythene tubing that can be sealed using heat — either an impulse sealer (the most common for small operations) or a continuous band sealer. For heat sealed plastic bags made from tubing, a basic hand-operated impulse sealer is sufficient for light duty. No complex equipment is required to start using lay-flat polythene tubing.
