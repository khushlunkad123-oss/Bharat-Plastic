Product Overview

About LDPE Shrink Film

Shrink film is a heat-shrinkable LDPE plastic film that, when wrapped around a product and exposed to heat, contracts tightly around the item to form a conforming protective seal. Bharat Plastic Manufacturing Co. manufactures LDPE shrink film rolls in Pimpri MIDC, Pune — supplying manufacturers, co-packers, FMCG companies and logistics operators across Maharashtra and India who require efficient, professional shrink packaging.

LDPE shrink film is used across industries for bundling, multi-packing and tamper-evident packaging of goods including beverages, dairy, consumer products, industrial components and construction materials. Our shrink film is available in clear and printed variants, in roll widths from 12 inches to 60+ inches, in thicknesses calibrated for the required shrink ratio and clarity. We supply shrink film for both hand-wrapping (with a heat gun) and machine-wrapping (on automatic shrink tunnels) applications.

Our LDPE shrink film is ideal for bundling, unitising and protecting industrial goods during transit and storage. When heat is applied using a heat gun or shrink tunnel, the film contracts uniformly to conform tightly to the product surface. We recommend approximately 100 microns thickness for best shrinking properties and reliable performance, though thinner films from 50 microns can also be produced.

LDPE shrink film is supplied in flat rolls in custom widths. It is widely used for pallet top sheeting, industrial bundling, machine wrapping and product protection. Custom roll widths and lengths are available on bulk orders.

FAQ

Common Questions

What thickness of LDPE shrink film do you recommend?
We can manufacture LDPE shrink film from 50 microns, but recommend approximately 100 microns thickness for reliable shrinking performance and good product protection. The ideal thickness depends on the size and weight of the product being wrapped.
What equipment is needed to use LDPE shrink film?
LDPE shrink film can be used with a heat gun for small-scale manual applications, or with a shrink tunnel for larger volume production lines. We can advise on compatible film widths and thicknesses for your existing equipment and application.
Do you supply LDPE shrink film in bulk to manufacturers in Maharashtra?
Yes. We supply LDPE shrink film in bulk to pharmaceutical, industrial and manufacturing companies across Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik and all of Maharashtra.
What is LDPE shrink film used for?
LDPE shrink film is used to bundle multiple products together (multi-packing), wrap individual products for tamper-evidence and surface protection, and unitise items on pallets. Common applications include beverage bottle multi-packs, dairy product trays, FMCG promotional packs, industrial part bundles, newspaper and magazine wrapping, and construction material protection.
What temperature is needed to activate LDPE shrink film?
LDPE shrink film typically requires heat between 120°C and 180°C to initiate shrinking. The exact temperature depends on the film thickness, the shrink rate required and the equipment used. Heat guns and heat tunnels both work effectively. For consistent results on production lines, we recommend using a calibrated shrink tunnel. Contact us for specific shrink temperature recommendations for our film grades.
