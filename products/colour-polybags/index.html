About Any Colour Polybags

Colour polybags are LDPE polythene bags manufactured in a range of colours including black, blue, red, green, yellow, white and any custom colour to your specification. Bharat Plastic Manufacturing Co. manufactures custom colour polybags in Pimpri MIDC, Pune — supplying industrial buyers, retailers and packaging companies across Maharashtra and India who require colour-coded or branded plastic bags.

Colour coding in plastic bags serves important functional purposes: black bags are widely used for waste segregation and light-sensitive storage; blue, red and green bags are used for waste management colour codes; white polybags are preferred for food-adjacent and hygiene-sensitive applications. We manufacture coloured LDPE bags in any size, thickness and custom colour, with the option to add printed logos or text. Bulk wholesale supply is available for all variants.

Colour coding in plastic bags helps identify products, materials, waste streams and handling instructions in manufacturing and logistics environments. Black LDPE bags are widely used for light-sensitive materials and opaque waste bags. Blue and red bags are common in medical and hazardous material segregation. White bags are used for clean room and food applications.

We produce coloured polybags by incorporating masterbatch into the LDPE during extrusion, ensuring consistent, uniform colour throughout the film.
