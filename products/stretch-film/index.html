About Stretch Film

Stretch film — also called pallet wrap or stretch wrap — is an elastic LLDPE (linear low density polyethylene) film that is stretched tightly around palletised loads, products and packages to secure, stabilise and protect them during storage and transport. Bharat Plastic Manufacturing Co. manufactures stretch film rolls in Pimpri MIDC, Pune, supplying warehouses, logistics companies, manufacturers and packaging distributors across Maharashtra and India.

We manufacture hand stretch film (for manual pallet wrapping) and machine stretch film (for use on semi-automatic and automatic stretch wrapping machines). Our stretch film provides excellent cling and load retention, preventing shifting and damage during transit. Available in transparent and black (light-blocking) variants, in roll widths of 400 mm, 500 mm and custom, and thicknesses of 50 microns and above as per Government of India norms. Bulk wholesale pricing is available for high-volume pallet wrap buyers.

Our stretch film is manufactured from LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene), which provides excellent cling, puncture resistance and load-holding force. We supply both hand-grade stretch film (for manual wrapping) and machine-grade stretch film (for automated pallet wrapping lines) in standard and custom widths.

Stretch film is widely used in automotive, pharmaceutical, FMCG, engineering and logistics sectors to protect wrapped pallets from dust, moisture and damage during transport. Our stretch film is supplied in bulk rolls at competitive factory-direct prices.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the difference between hand stretch film and machine stretch film?
Hand stretch film is designed for manual application — it is typically 400–500 mm wide, 50 microns or above as per Government norms, and wound on a cardboard core sized for hand dispensers. Machine stretch film is used on semi-automatic or automatic pallet wrappers — it is wound on narrower cores, applied at higher pre-stretch ratios (up to 250–300%) to reduce film consumption and cost per pallet. We supply both grades — contact us to specify your wrapping method.
What thickness of stretch film should I use for heavy industrial pallets?
For heavy or sharp-edged loads, we recommend 80–100 microns. For standard loads, 50–70 microns is usually sufficient. Contact us to discuss your specific load requirement.
Do you supply stretch film in bulk to warehouses across Maharashtra?
Yes. We supply stretch film rolls in bulk to manufacturers, warehouse operators and logistics companies across Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik, Aurangabad and all of Maharashtra.
How much stretch film is needed to wrap one pallet?
Typical pallet wrapping uses 200–400 grams of stretch film per standard pallet depending on load stability requirements, film thickness and stretch ratio applied. A standard 50-micron, 500 mm wide roll will typically wrap 4–8 pallets depending on the pre-stretch ratio applied. Machine stretch film at higher pre-stretch ratios significantly reduces film consumption per pallet. Contact us to calculate the right quantity for your pallet dimensions and load type.
